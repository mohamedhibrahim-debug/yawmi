2. iPhone app and voice

Done in code
  • Say your tasks in Egyptian Arabic; review them on simple cards before adding
  • Phone-style app with a microphone button in the middle
  • iOS project with alarms that ring when a wait ends, even with the app closed
3

3. First iPhone build

Nowabout 1 week
  • Build on a Mac or a cloud Mac, and install through TestFlight
  • Check that Apple's speech recognition understands Egyptian Arabic
  • Fix the two known bugs: finishing a task stops its wait, and yesterday's tasks vanish
  • Bring over the best parts of the earlier Codex version, then retire it

Ready when: the app runs on a real iPhone, voice works in Egyptian Arabic, and a wait alarm rings with the app closed.

4

4. Test with 8–10 women

Next2 weeks
  • Do they open it every day?
  • Is voice easier than typing?
  • Do "effort minutes" and waiting time make sense to them?

Passes if: at least half use it on 6 of 10 days, and most add tasks by voice without help.

5. Launch or rethink

Decisionabout 2 weeks
  • If the test passes: publish on the App Store and add iCloud backup
  • If it doesn't: simplify to light / medium / heavy days and a plan she can share on WhatsApp
Needed from you for step 3
  • An Apple Developer account ($99 a year) to install on iPhones and use TestFlight
  • A Mac with Xcode, or permission to build on GitHub's cloud Macs
Later, only if women ask for it

Sharing with a partner or kids, an Android version, sync between devices, shopping lists.
